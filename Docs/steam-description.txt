[h1]ProgressionLESS: Gravship[/h1]
[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3522893553]Progression: Gravship[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Keeps the grav engine from falling until your colony reaches the Spacer era.
[*]Holds back the gravship quest line until then.
[*]Sets the gravtech research projects to Spacer tech level.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Recent versions of Progression: Gravship add up to [b]20 extra research projects[/b] alongside Vanilla Gravship Expanded, and rename and rearrange the gravtech tree. This fork removes all of them.
[list]
[*][b]12 projects[/b] removed with Vanilla Gravship Expanded alone.
[*][b]Up to 8 more[/b] removed with Chapter 2, Gravship Storage, Vehicle Map Framework or Dubs Bad Hygiene.
[*]Vanilla Gravship Expanded's research tree stays exactly as that mod made it.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Progression: Scenarios
[*]Debt-Bound Gravship
[*]Orbital Station Start
[*]Vanilla Gravship Expanded (Chapters 1 and 2)
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]
